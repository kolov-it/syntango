# Past participles of essere verbs that also name a state after essere.
# After essere, the participle of a verb that takes essere is the compound
# tense (sono venuto, è successo), not an adjective: vp.copula refuses it.
# These are both: "è morto" is "he died" and "he is dead". Their participle
# keeps the adjective use after essere too; it is still the verb's
# participle.
#
# The lexicon's adjective entries cannot tell these apart: venuto, arrivato,
# caduto, partito and nato have one as well, and after essere are only the
# compound tense (nato as an adjective, "a born leader", goes with a noun:
# un leader nato, not è nato).
#
# One participle per line, masculine singular.
morto
